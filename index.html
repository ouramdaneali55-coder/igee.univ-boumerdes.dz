About IGEE

An institute looking to the future, combining quality education and cutting-edge research.

Our history, our mission

Heir to INELEC, the Institute of Electrical and Electronic Engineering (IGEE) became a full-fledged university institution in 2010.

Our mission is to train highly qualified engineers and researchers in the fields of electronics, telecommunications, automatic control and electrical engineering. All our programs are taught entirely in English.

  • Nationally recognized excellence in education
  • Highly qualified teaching staff
  • Well-equipped research laboratories
  • Industrial and international partnerships

Graduation

First-cycle programs: Bachelor's and Master's.

Graduation Ceremony 2025 — IGEE Class

Bachelor's

The Bachelor's in Electrical and Electronic Engineering is a generalist degree designed to provide a solid basic education.

At the end of this program, students will have followed fundamental courses in electrical circuits, electronics and computer science.

Master's

The graduation cycle offers two cycles of study: Bachelor's and Master's.

Master's graduates can access doctoral training after passing the national exam.

Postgraduate Studies

Third-cycle programs: Magister, DPGS and Doctorate.

Opening Conditions

To open a postgraduate program:

  • Have a functional diploma program
  • Have three (03) senior-ranked teachers
  • Have two (02) research teams
  • A minimum of six open positions

Systems and Degrees

The postgraduate cycle is divided into two systems:

  • The LMD system
  • The classical system: MAGISTER or DPGS, then DOCTORATE

The Scientific Council gives its opinion on orientations related to scientific research and higher education programs.
